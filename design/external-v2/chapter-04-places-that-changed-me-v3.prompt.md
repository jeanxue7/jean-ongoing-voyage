## Creative direction — From the Road to the Sky

The following morning, Jean continues alone and watches sunrise from one hot-air balloon above the East African savanna. Echo the Opening through composition rather than repeated action: Jean remains anchored on the right in her red shell, mustard beanie and chartreuse trousers, with a vast cobalt-to-apricot sky opening on the left. She no longer walks or looks back; she faces the new horizon.

Place the camera just outside the basket's left-front corner with a 26–28 mm editorial lens. The realistic wicker basket fills the lower-right corner; restrained suspension cables, a cropped terracotta canopy edge and a small burner flame remain confined to the far right. Show Jean from mid-thigh upward in calm three-quarter profile. Her sunglasses rest on her beanie. Her pale-blue backpack is secured upright inside the basket. One visible hand rests naturally on the rail; the far-side arm remains hidden.

Below, show a believable East African Rift savanna: gold-brown grass, sparse umbrella acacias, a misted seasonal river forming an S through the landscape and distant blue-violet escarpments. Place a small sunrise low near the left-center horizon. Reserve the upper-left 40% as a low-detail gray-blue, pale-apricot and rose dawn gradient with no balloon parts, birds, peaks or flare.

Use the established premium cinematic stylized-3D realism, tactile materials, fine film grain and restrained saturation. Jean's vermilion jacket is the only strong accent. Exclude other people, pilots, passengers, other balloons, rainbow canopies, safari-ad clichés, tourist poses, focal wildlife, giant sun, dramatic rays, snow mountains, collage, text, logo, watermark and UI.

## Refinement

The final precise edit removed a disconnected hand from the top of the backpack and reconstructed the affected pale-blue fabric, webbing and buckle. Jean's visible hand on the basket rail, pose, identity, landscape, balloon structure and copy space were preserved.

## Final QA

- Exactly one person: Jean.
- Hot-air-balloon structure reads immediately without becoming a postcard cliché.
- Opening composition and signature colors return in a new morning context.
- East African dawn and upper-left copy space remain clear.
- No anatomical anomaly, baked-in typography, logo, watermark or UI.
