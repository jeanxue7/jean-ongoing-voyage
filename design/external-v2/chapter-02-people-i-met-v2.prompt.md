## Creative direction

Create a candid creek-meadow lunch rather than another walking scene. Use exactly the same three hikers throughout Chapters 02–04: Jean, a woman friend with a high messy bun and teal shell, and a man friend with a beige cap and rust shell. Jean sits cross-legged in the center holding a blue enamel bowl; the woman friend stirs a pot on a compact canister stove; the man friend eats flatbread beside them. Place one small orange tent and Jean's pale-blue pack behind the group. Keep the sparse forest, meadow and creek believable and quiet.

Jean retains her mustard beanie, short brown bob, sunglasses pushed onto the beanie, vermilion-red shell, chartreuse trousers and dark boots. Match the established premium cinematic stylized-3D realism, tactile outdoor materials, natural late-afternoon light and restrained outdoor-campaign finish.

Use a low seated eye-level 35 mm composition. Keep the three people and cooking action in the left and central 62–65% of the frame. Reserve the right 35–38% for later webpage copy, with soft meadow, a low distant ridge and pale blue sky; avoid people, tent, branches, bright reflections and high-frequency detail there. No extra hikers, campfire, smoke plume, labels, UI, watermark or malformed hands.

## Refinement

The final regional edit simplified only the right side into a lower-detail meadow, subdued ridge and open sky while preserving the trio, stove, food, tent, creek, identities, lighting and crop.
