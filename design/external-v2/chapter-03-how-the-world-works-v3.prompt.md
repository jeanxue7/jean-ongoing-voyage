# Chapter 03 / How the World Works — v3 generation record

## Intended use

- One independent 16:9 full-bleed website hero image.
- HTML copy is added separately; the image contains no typography or interface.
- The matte bottle-green wall on the left is the primary copy-safe area.

## Creative direction — After the Conversation

Create an entirely empty vintage mountain tavern immediately after three friends have left. Tell the story through objects rather than people: exactly three subtly mismatched short glasses at different fill levels, one dog-eared open book, two softly creased letter pages, a torn-open aged envelope and a simple wooden pencil. Condensation, wet glass rings and shifted papers should make the scene feel recently lived in rather than staged.

Shoot from seated eye level with a 40–45 mm lens, looking diagonally across a worn dark-wood table. Keep the narrative objects in the lower-right and middle-right. Reserve the left 38–42% as a quiet smoke-softened bottle-green plaster wall with only gentle tonal falloff. A worn oxblood leather banquette sits in the right rear, with a narrow cobalt-blue twilight window and restrained amber side light.

The palette is dominated by desaturated bottle green, tobacco wood and shadow; oxblood red is a secondary structural accent rather than an equal color block. Use premium cinematic editorial still-life photography with tactile stylized realism, physically convincing glass, paper and leather, medium depth of field and fine film grain. The mood is a thoughtful postscript after a long conversation.

Strictly exclude people, hands, human reflections, bartender, crowd, flat-lay framing, glossy liquor advertising, hero bottles, readable labels, festive red-green styling, Christmas cues, literal globes/maps/gears, excessive props, readable generated writing, text, logo, watermark and UI.

## Final QA

- No people appear.
- Exactly three glasses plus restrained books-and-letters storytelling.
- Bottle green dominates; oxblood red remains controlled.
- Left copy area is calm and dark enough for light HTML copy.
- No baked-in typography, logo, watermark or UI.
