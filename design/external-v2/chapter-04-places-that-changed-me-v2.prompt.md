Show one coherent moment at the end of the following day, not a split scene or time montage. The same three friends have climbed from a cool misty morning and now pause together on a high summit shelf at sunset beside a visibly unfinished mountain road. Avoid another ordinary walking composition.

Place the trio left-of-center in lively three-quarter profile. Jean, in her mustard beanie, vermilion shell and chartreuse trousers, sits briefly on a summit rock and laughs toward her friends; her sunglasses are pushed onto the beanie and her pale-blue backpack has just been set beside her. The teal-jacket woman stands close at Jean's left with one hand naturally at a pack strap. The rust-jacket man stands one step higher, smiling and pointing toward the road ahead. Keep exactly three people and preserve the established faces, hair, outfits and chemistry.

Use a wide 28 mm trail-level environmental composition. A rough mountain route enters from the lower foreground, bends in an S past the group and continues beyond the ridge. Far below, cool silver-blue mist pools in the valley as a subtle memory of the morning departure; a small sunset sits at the far-left horizon and gives the group warm golden rim light. Keep the upper-right 32–35% as low-detail blue-to-lavender sky for future copy, free of people, peaks, branches, birds and bright flare.

Maintain premium cinematic stylized-3D realism, natural material detail and an optimistic amber/cobalt palette. No extra people, flag, jumping, raised-fist summit pose, construction machinery, workers, signs, collage, duplicate figures, text, logos, UI or watermark.
