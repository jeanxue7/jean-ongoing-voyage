Natural terrain, no fantasy geography, no tourist signage, no arrows, no fences, no buildings.

Subject: only one Jean hiker. Show her full body at the lower-right quadrant in three-quarter back or side view, actively walking toward the fork. Style: high-end cinematic stylized realism, a refined PBR 3D digital character seamlessly integrated into a photoreal natural environment.
